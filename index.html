About RK TRADERS

RK TRADERS is a trusted stationery store located in Karicode, Kollam, directly catering to students, engineering aspirants at TKM College, local offices, and businesses. We provide high-quality stationery products for school students, college students, engineering students, architecture students, offices, and businesses. Our goal is to offer a wide variety of products at affordable prices with excellent customer service.

Differentiators

Why Choose RK TRADERS?

We offer more than just stationery; we build relations based on trust, quality, and exceptional local accessibility.

Premium Quality

We stock stationery from leading national and international brands like Classmate, Faber-Castell, Rotring, Casio, and Solo, ensuring durability and reliability.

Best Student Rates

Being adjacent to colleges and school zones, we offer highly competitive retail and wholesale prices tailored specifically for student budgets.

Massive Collection

From simple pencils and notebook registers to sophisticated mini-drafters, drafting tables, model-making boards, and scientific calculators, we have it all.

Academic Specialization

Specialized drawing templates, tracing rolls, architectural boards, and high-quality craft elements catering directly to engineering and architecture programs.

Convenient Spot

Perfect location in Karicode on TKM College Road, Kollam. Stop by easily during college hours or order via call for quick pick-up.

Cooperative Service

Need a particular product or material that's hard to find? Tell us, and our friendly staff will order or source it specifically for your project.
